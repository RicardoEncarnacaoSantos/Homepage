Artificial Intelligence

Yann LeCun on Quora
Currently Director of AI Research at Facebook and Professor at NYU, father of convolutional models, Yann LeCun is definitely a guru to follow.
Andrew Ng on Quora
Another big name to follow. Andrew Ng is Chief Scientist of Baidu and Adjunct Professor at Stanford University. He founded and led the "Google Brain" project, resulting in the famous "Google Cat" deep learning model that was able to recognize cats in YouTube videos, back in 2012.
Paul Tardy on Quora
Interesting Q&A here too!
Ethical Issues in Advanced Artificial Intelligence
A philosophical paper about the need of ethics in a possible Artificial Superintelligence, by Nick Bostrom (Professor at Oxford and Director of the Future of Humanity Institute). You can find here the Paperclip Maximizer example, which I personally like.

Deep Reinforcement Learning

Deep Reinforcement Learning (DeepMind)
A DeepMind post about Deep RL using the Deep Q-Network algorithm (DQN), created by DeepMind, a 2014 breakthrough where the same algorithm is able to learn to play dozens of Atari games from raw sets of pixels.
Universe (Open AI)
From Open AI, an attempt to create a generic reinforcement learning platform to build systems capable of learning from screen pixels.
Gym (Open AI)
Want to see if your latest RL algorithm is better than others? Open AI offers dozens of game environments that you can you use for benchmarking.
